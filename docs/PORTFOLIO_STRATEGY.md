# Portfolio Strategy

## Positioning

Primary position: **Quality & Release Engineering Leader · AI & Agentic Systems Builder**.

The site is written for CTOs, engineering leaders, quality leaders and technical recruiters. It connects leadership to executable systems rather than presenting a list of tools or fabricated proficiency percentages.

## Narrative

1. Establish the quality and agentic-engineering position.
2. Explain AI acceleration inside explicit controls.
3. Prove the position through six public engineering projects.
4. Show quality, release and agentic operating models.
5. Close with leadership principles, resume truthfulness and verified contact channels.

## Evidence policy

- Claims must be supported by public repository content.
- Product limitations remain visible where they affect interpretation.
- No private platform content, internal company information, customer data or fabricated metrics.
- FlowProof links to Architecture because no dedicated public case study was verified.
- Shabi's AI Academy explicitly discloses its browser-local and disconnected-provider boundary.
- The data-quality project is described as a synthetic reference pipeline, not a production data connection.
- LinkedIn and email were verified on 2026-08-14 against the public `Shabi-Resume/index.html`; the exposed phone number is intentionally excluded.

## Visual strategy

Deep navy, restrained cyan/blue accents, strong typographic hierarchy and purposeful cards convey engineering leadership without stock imagery or decorative motion. Layouts are designed for desktop, laptop, tablet, mobile and small-mobile widths.
