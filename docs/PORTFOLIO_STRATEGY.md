# Portfolio Strategy

## Positioning

Primary position: **Quality & Release Engineering Leader · AI & Agentic Systems Builder**.

The landing page is designed for CTOs, engineering leaders, quality leaders and technical recruiters. A first-time visitor should understand Shabi's identity, operating model, strongest work and next actions within the first screen.

## Narrative

1. Establish identity and value with a strong editorial Hero.
2. Prove the positioning through FlowProof, CWL Office and Agentic Engineering Platform.
3. Provide four smaller public engineering showcases without equal visual weight.
4. Connect strategic leadership to hands-on engineering depth.
5. Summarize only four verified career signals.
6. Explain Prompt, Context, Agentic Engineering, Evaluation and Governance in one compact section and flow.
7. Close with verified contact channels and a working CV download.

The previous standalone Quality, Agentic, Leadership, Principles and Resume sections were merged into this shorter narrative to remove repetition.

## Evidence policy

- Claims must be supported by current repository content or the intentionally public resume source.
- FlowProof version detail is omitted from the page so the landing copy does not age.
- CWL Office is represented prominently as private-source engineering with no repository link.
- The private central capability repository is never linked or described beyond the public operating model.
- Public project links are explicit and use current repository/case-study URLs.
- Metrics retain the exact qualification used by the public resume source.
- English and Hebrew copy are authored separately; Hebrew keeps established engineering terms in English where that is clearer for an Israeli technology audience.
- LinkedIn, GitHub and email are the only published contact channels; the legacy phone number is excluded.

## Visual strategy

The system evolves the prior public resume direction: charcoal and navy surfaces, restrained gold editorial emphasis, teal verification cues, a serif display hierarchy and technical metadata in monospace. Natural scrolling, generous whitespace and a clear featured-versus-more-project distinction replace portal-like density.

The intentionally public 700×896 portrait is reused without generative alteration. Fonts remain local system fallbacks to protect privacy and performance. Section spacing uses `--section-space: clamp(52px, 6vw, 92px)` and a smaller mobile range so adjacent content remains visually connected without losing breathing room.
